      Turn any paper into a checked question pack

      PDF (typed or scanned), screenshots and photos, Word, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, HTML.

      1. Upload a paper. The program measures it and writes a profiling brief with page images.
      2. Give the brief and the images to any AI model. It replies with one YAML profile. Paste it here, Lint and Try until clean.
      3. Run: the program converts the paper with no AI and runs six independent checks.
      4. Review the packet (some items are corrupted on purpose), approve, and download the pack.
